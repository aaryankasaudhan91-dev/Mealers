Campus lunch should be simple, but kitchens often prepare before they know where people will eat. Mealers helps, one caterer, coordinate choices across nearby campus kitchens before cooking begins. A diner checks the meal, walking distance, and declared allergens, then confirms a suitable option. At pickup, staff validate the booking code. Kitchen managers see committed demand, protect a walk-in buffer, and approve preparation quantities. They record collection and leftovers separately. Who pays? Our proposed customer is the campus caterer. The business model is a monthly subscription per kitchen. Priced in rupees. The price still needs validation. Forecasting and pre-order tools already exist. Our focus is voluntary coordination between nearby kitchens. A pilot must show whether that creates useful value after software costs and extra staff effort. We would start with two kitchens, comparing leftovers, shortages, and pickup completion against a baseline. Mealers is a synthetic MVP, customers, revenue, and savings remain unproven. The next step is an operator pilot.
